# An AI powered Website Audit tool

Somewhere right now, a small business owner just spent their savings building a website, hit publish, and is waiting for customers who are not coming.

They do not know why. Maybe the headline does not say what the business actually does. Maybe the call to action is buried below three sections nobody scrolls to. Maybe the whole page just feels cluttered in a way they cannot quite put into words. They are not a designer, so they cannot diagnose it themselves, and hiring an agency to tell them what is wrong costs more money than they have room for right now.

This happens constantly. Websites lose visitors every day for reasons nobody ever points out, because proper feedback is expensive and slow, and the tools that are free, like Lighthouse or PageSpeed Insights, only measure technical performance. They can tell you your page loads in two seconds. They cannot tell you your headline is confusing, your CTA is invisible, or your layout is quietly pushing people away.

That gap between what free tools measure and what actually makes a website work is where most small websites get stuck.

## What We Are Building

SiteIQ is our attempt to close that gap.

A user uploads a screenshot of their website, or gives us a URL, along with a short description of their business and who they are trying to reach. From there, the system does the work an expensive audit would normally do. It runs technical checks where a clear pass or fail rule applies, and it uses AI to look at the things that actually require judgment, the layout, the visual hierarchy, whether the copy is persuasive, whether the page guides someone toward taking action.

The output is a single report that tells the user exactly what is wrong, why it matters, and what to change, the same kind of feedback they would otherwise have to pay for and wait days to receive.

## How It Works

1. The user uploads a screenshot of their website, and optionally a URL
2. They fill in a short form about their business, their audience, and their goal for the site
3. The system runs three layers of analysis
   - Rule based checks for technical elements like SEO tags, heading structure, and alt text
   - AI vision analysis on the screenshot to evaluate layout, hierarchy, spacing, and CTA visibility
   - AI text analysis on the extracted content to evaluate clarity and persuasiveness
4. All findings are combined into one structured report with severity levels and specific recommendations
5. The user can review it on a dashboard or download it as a PDF

We presented the project for the first time. At that point it was a fairly early idea, an AI powered tool that could take a website and return UI/UX and content feedback.

Dr. Hassam liked the direction but felt it was too thin as presented. He asked us to think harder about the feature set and come back with a detailed explanation of how the system would actually work end to end, not just the general concept. The instruction was clear, stop pitching and start documenting.

### September 21, 2026

We returned with a full written brief, RAW FYP DETAILED BRIEF, covering the project vision, the feature set, the technical workflow, the AI integration plan, and a first pass at the system architecture.
